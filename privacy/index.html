Privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Basics Lives Kit is a private tool run by the owner of the Business Basics channel for their own use.

Google user data

The app asks for access to Google Docs (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/documents) only so it can add new tabs with show notes to the owner's own Google Doc. It does not read, collect, sell or share any other Google data.

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer and in the owner's private cloud workspace settings. It is never sent to anyone else.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other data

The app keeps no analytics, cookies or personal information about visitors or users.
